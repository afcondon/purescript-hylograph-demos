machine  washing-machine
title    "Washing machine"
format   2
initial  idle

STATES           LABEL       ON ENTRY            ON EXIT
  idle           Idle
  locking        Locking     lock-door
  prewashing     Prewash     fill-drum, tumble
  filling        Filling     fill-drum
  washing        Washing     heat-water, tumble
  draining       Draining    drain
  rinse-filling  Rinse fill  fill-drum
  rinsing        Rinsing     tumble
  spinning       Spinning    drain, spin
  unlocking      Unlocking   unlock-door
  done           Done        chime
  paused         Paused      hold-everything

EVENTS       SOURCE   LABEL
  start      user
  pause      user
  resume     user
  cancel     user
  open       user     open door
  step-done  runtime  step complete

CONFIG         DEFAULT  LABEL
  has-prewash  false    Prewash in this programme

FACTS              MEANING
  door-shut        The door is shut
  more-rinses      The controller wants another rinse
  drum-wet         There is water in the drum
  paused-in-fill   Paused while filling
  paused-in-rinse  Paused while rinsing

REFUSALS       MEANING
  door-ajar    the door is not shut
  door-locked  the door is locked while the drum is running
  still-wet    the drum must drain before the door opens
  not-running  nothing is running

COMMANDS           MEANING
  lock-door        Lock the door
  unlock-door      Unlock the door
  fill-drum        Fill the drum
  heat-water       Heat the water
  tumble           Tumble the drum
  drain            Drain the drum
  spin             Spin
  hold-everything  Hold everything where it is
  chime            Sound the end-of-cycle chime

GRID   rows are states, columns are events
                   start    pause         resume        cancel     open          step-done
  > idle           [1]      !not-running  !not-running  .          .             .
    locking        .        paused        .             unlocking  !door-locked  [2]
    prewashing     .        paused        .             draining   !door-locked  draining
    filling        .        paused        .             draining   !door-locked  washing
    washing        .        paused        .             draining   !door-locked  draining
    draining       .        .             .             .          !door-locked  [3]
    rinse-filling  .        paused        .             draining   !door-locked  rinsing
    rinsing        .        paused        .             draining   !door-locked  draining
    spinning       .        paused        .             unlocking  !door-locked  unlocking
    unlocking      .        .             .             .          !door-locked  [4]
    done *         locking  !not-running  !not-running  .          idle          .
    paused         .        .             [5]           draining   !door-locked  .

KEY
  >     the initial state
  *     a final state
  .     stay — the event is declared here and does nothing
  !tag  refuse with that tag — see REFUSALS
  [n]   guarded, or declared twice — see CASES

CASES
  [1]  idle on start
         when not door-shut    refuse door-ajar
         else                  -> locking
  [2]  locking on step-done
         when has-prewash      -> prewashing
         else                  -> filling
  [3]  draining on step-done
         when more-rinses      -> rinse-filling
         else                  -> spinning
  [4]  unlocking on step-done
         when drum-wet         refuse still-wet
         else                  -> done
  [5]  paused on resume
         when paused-in-rinse  -> rinsing
         elif paused-in-fill   -> filling
         else                  -> washing
