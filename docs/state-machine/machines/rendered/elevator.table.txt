machine  elevator
title    "Elevator"
format   2
initial  idle

STATES        LABEL           ON ENTRY                  ON EXIT
  idle        Idle            close-doors, hold-brake
  up          Going up        close-doors, drive-up
  down        Going down      close-doors, drive-down
  arriving    Arriving        stop-drive, ring-arrival
  doors-open  Doors open      open-doors
  held        Doors held      hold-doors
  stopped     Emergency stop  stop-drive, sound-alarm   cancel-alarm

EVENTS     SOURCE   LABEL
  call     user
  open     user     open doors
  close    user     close doors
  alarm    user     emergency stop
  reset    user
  reached  runtime  floor reached
  dwelt    runtime  dwell elapsed

CONFIG             DEFAULT  LABEL
  dwell-seconds    6        Doors stay open for (s)
  has-hold-button  true     This car has a hold button

FACTS            MEANING
  wanted-above   A floor above is requested
  wanted-below   A floor below is requested
  doors-blocked  Something is in the doorway

REFUSALS          MEANING
  nowhere-to-go   no floor is requested
  moving          the car is moving
  blocked         something is in the doorway
  no-hold-button  this car has no hold button
  stopped-fast    the emergency stop is engaged

COMMANDS        MEANING
  hold-brake    Set the parking brake
  drive-up      Drive the car up
  drive-down    Drive the car down
  stop-drive    Stop the drive
  ring-arrival  Ring the arrival chime
  open-doors    Open the doors
  close-doors   Close the doors
  hold-doors    Hold the doors open
  sound-alarm   Sound the alarm
  cancel-alarm  Silence the alarm

GRID   rows are states, columns are events
                call           open           close          alarm    reset       reached   dwelt
  > idle        [1]            doors-open     .              stopped  .           .         .
    up          .              !moving        .              stopped  .           arriving  .
    down        .              !moving        .              stopped  .           arriving  .
    arriving    .              doors-open     .              stopped  .           .         doors-open
    doors-open  .              [2]            [3]            stopped  .           .         @[4]
    held        .              .              doors-open     stopped  .           .         .
    stopped     !stopped-fast  !stopped-fast  !stopped-fast  .        doors-open  .         .

KEY
  >     the initial state
  .     stay — the event is declared here and does nothing
  !tag  refuse with that tag — see REFUSALS
  [n]   guarded, or declared twice — see CASES
  @     also fires by itself — see DEADLINES

CASES
  [1]  idle on call
         when wanted-above         -> up
         elif wanted-below         -> down
         else                      refuse nowhere-to-go
  [2]  doors-open on open
         when not has-hold-button  refuse no-hold-button
         else                      -> held
  [3]  doors-open on close
         when doors-blocked        refuse blocked
         elif wanted-above         -> up
         elif wanted-below         -> down
         else                      -> idle
  [4]  doors-open on dwelt
         when doors-blocked        stay
         elif wanted-above         -> up
         elif wanted-below         -> down
         else                      -> idle

DEADLINES
  doors-open  after dwell-seconds  fires dwelt
