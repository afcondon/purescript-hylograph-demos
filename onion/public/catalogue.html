hylograph-onion

Visual encoding catalogue — 10 dimensions for watercolour data visualisation

Size

Continuous. Radius of the base shape.

Shape

Categorical. Base polygon geometry.

Colour

Categorical or continuous. Fill hue.

Fuzziness

Continuous. Edge diffusion — encodes uncertainty.

Opacity

Continuous. Layer intensity — encodes confidence.

Gradient

Structural. Internal colour variation.

Orientation

Continuous (angular). Rotation of asymmetric shapes.

Aspect ratio

Continuous. Width-to-height ratio of ellipse base.

Palette

Categorical (higher-order). Constrains colour range.

Combinations

Multiple dimensions composed on single marks.
